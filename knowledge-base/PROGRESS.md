## Conventions (decided 2026-10-02)
- doc_id = SOURCE-TOPIC-NNN; NNN is a plain counter per prefix, not the week number. Prefixes: MLP (MedlinePlus); NHS-<HUB> for NHS: LABOUR, COMPLICATIONS, SYMPTOMS, EXISTING, FINDING, LGBT, WELL, MENTAL, TRYING, CARE, COND, TESTS, BABY, VACC.
- source_org uses one fixed spelling per source: "MedlinePlus (US National Library of Medicine)", "NHS (UK)".
- WHO India and UNICEF India files use region: india.
- week_start / week_end are copied exactly as the source states them; trimester follows the guide's table; any mismatch is explained in notes.
- date_accessed = the day the text was actually downloaded/opened; date_checked = the day the terms were read.
- published_or_reviewed is quoted text (YYYY, YYYY-MM or YYYY-MM-DD).
- Originals: raw/medlineplus/ and raw/nhs/.

- contains_treatment_advice: true if the text says someone should take, use or have a specific medicine, supplement, vaccine, injection or procedure (including prevention), or may need one; false if it only explains or gives general advice; when unsure choose true.
- Text must be copied word for word. Allowed body changes: removing page clutter per guide 4.4, and making a file match the live page (explain in notes).
- Licence rule R4: if reuse permission is unclear, treat as Tier C and do not put it in clean/. Copying by hand does not change this.
