# Cookie and Storage Notice — Alovia (DRAFT)

Shown at `/cookies`. Review with the supervisor before publication.

Alovia uses only **essential** browser storage:

| What | Why | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Sign-in session (from our sign-in provider, Supabase) | Keeps you signed in | Browser local storage |
| Theme (light/dark) and "reduce motion" | Remembers your display choice | Browser local storage |
| App files for the installable version (patient app) | Lets the app open quickly from your home screen | Browser cache |

We do **not** use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not load third-party trackers. Because only essential storage is used, there is no cookie banner. If this ever changes, we will ask for your consent first.

You can clear this storage in your browser settings; you will be signed out.
