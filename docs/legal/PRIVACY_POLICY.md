## 1. Who we are
Alovia is a **research prototype** built as an academic capstone project by [team names] at [MJCET, Hyderabad], supervised by [supervisor]. Contact for privacy questions: [project email].

Alovia is **not a medical service** and is **not for clinical use**. During the project, only test or synthetic data should be entered.

## 2. What we collect and why

| Data | Why | Who enters it |
|---|---|---|
| Email and password (handled by our sign-in provider) | To create and secure your account | You |
| Confirmation that you are 18 or older; your consent and its date | Legal requirement and record of consent | You |
| Name (first name is enough), optional age | To identify your record for your doctor | You or your doctor |
| Pregnancy dates (last period, due date, scan dates) | To show your pregnancy journey | You or your doctor |
| Symptoms you log, with notes | To give safety prompts and show your history to you and your doctor | You |
| Genetic information about you and your baby's father | To calculate educational inheritance estimates | You (your doctor may correct it) |
| Appointments | To show your calendar | You or your doctor |
| Clinical values entered by your doctor (CTG, vitals) and AI predictions | To support your doctor's decisions | Your doctor |
| Medical files uploaded by your doctor | To keep your records together | Your doctor |
| Questions to the knowledge assistant (when available) | To answer your questions and improve safety | You |

We do **not** collect phone numbers, addresses, government ID numbers or payment details. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

## 3. Who can see your data
- **You.**
- **Doctors assigned to you** in Alovia.
- **Administrators** can see account details and assignments, **not** your health information.
- **Service providers** that host the system: Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage), Render (server), Vercel (website hosting), and — only when the AI features are switched on — an AI service provider [name], which receives text **without your name or ID**.

We do not sell or share your data for marketing.

## 4. Your baby's father's information
If you enter information about the baby's father, please make sure he agrees. It is used only for the genetic estimate.

## 5. How long we keep data
While your account exists. You can ask us to delete it at any time (§6). [Retention for inactive accounts: to be decided.]

## 6. Your choices and rights
- See your data in the app.
- Correct your pregnancy dates and appointments; ask your doctor to correct clinical data.
- **Request deletion:** Settings → "Request deletion of my data". We complete it within [30] days.
- Withdraw consent by requesting deletion.
- Contact: [project email]. If you are not satisfied, you may contact the Data Protection Board of India.

## 7. Security
Data is sent over encrypted connections, stored with our hosting providers, and only our server can access the database. No system is perfectly secure; please do not enter real health information while Alovia is a prototype.

## 8. Children
Alovia is for people aged **18 or older**.

## 9. Changes
We will show a notice in the app when this policy changes, and ask for consent again if the change affects how we use your data.
