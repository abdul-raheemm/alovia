## 0. How to use this document (read first)

These rules apply to anyone building from this document, including AI coding assistants.

1. **Use only what is written here.** Screens, states, calculations, data fields and rules are defined in this file.
2. **Never invent medical content.** No fetal-development text, size, symptom description or advice may be written from memory or generated by an AI model. Content comes only from the content provider (section 8), and every item carries its source.
3. **Tags:**
   - `[CONFIGURABLE]`: a setting in `config.yaml`, never hard-coded.
   - `[OPEN]`: not decided. Leave a clear placeholder; do not guess.
   - `[CONFIRM AT REVIEW]`: the reviewing doctor must confirm before release.
4. **Patient-facing wording is not written here.** Only message keys and intent (section 13). Final text lives in `messages.yaml`, approved by a doctor.
5. **Phone numbers are never written from memory.** They are copied from an official page (section 13.3).
6. **One word, one meaning.** Use the glossary terms exactly.

## 1. Purpose and scope

### 1.1 What the module is
The Pregnancy Journey is the **home screen of the patient app**. It is the first thing the patient sees after signing in. It shows where she is in her pregnancy, what is typical at this stage, her calendar, and quick access to every other patient feature. Its design is inspired by card-based cycle-tracking apps (Clue is the reference named in the original module write-up).

### 1.2 What it does
- Collects pregnancy dates and calculates gestational age and the estimated due date.
- Shows a home screen with week and day, a month illustration, a baby-size comparison and stage content.
- Shows 9 month cards with detail views.
- Shows a pregnancy calendar: milestones, appointments and symptom days.
- Embeds Symptom Tracking (Module 7): quick log, urgent button, symptom days on the calendar.
- Shows in-app notifications in a notification bar, and a nav bar to all patient features.
- Handles the end of a pregnancy (birth or loss) gently and safely.

### 1.3 What it never does
- Never generates medical content with AI.
- Never shows an illustration or size as "your baby". Always "a typical baby at this stage".
- Never animates a heartbeat or anything that looks like live monitoring data.
- Never continues baby content, celebrations or weekly notifications after a pregnancy is marked as ended.
- Never sends push notifications in version 1 (in-app only).

## 2. Glossary

| Term | Meaning in this module |
|---|---|
| **LMP** | First day of the last menstrual period. |
| **due date** | Estimated due date stored in `patients.due_date`. Source of truth for all calculations. |
| **due date source** | How the due date was set: `lmp_estimate`, `clinician_confirmed`, `ultrasound`. |
| **gestational age (GA)** | Days since the start of pregnancy by LMP convention: `GA_days = 280 − (due_date − today)`. |
| **completed weeks** | `floor(GA_days / 7)`. Displayed as "N weeks, D days pregnant", where `D = GA_days mod 7`. |
| **content week** | The completed weeks value used to select content (an "at 24 weeks" page matches completed weeks = 24). |
| **pregnancy month** | Display label 1–9 from the content week (section 6.3). Never stored. |
| **trimester** | `1` (weeks 0–13), `2` (14–27), `3` (28 and above), same as the knowledge base guide. |
| **pregnancy status** | `active`, `ended_birth`, `ended_loss`, `ended_not_specified`. |
| **content item** | One piece of stage content (title, body, source) from the content provider. |
| **content provider** | The code component that supplies content items (section 8). |
| **prototype content** | Small test content from web-searched trusted sources, used until the knowledge base is ready (section 8.3). |
| **size reference** | A table row giving a typical size for a week, with its source, and the comparison object shown. |
| **notification** | An in-app message shown in the notification bar. |

## 5. Onboarding and pregnancy dates

### 5.1 Input options (patient chooses one)

| Option | Patient enters | Stored `due_date` | `due_date_source` |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | LMP date | LMP + 280 days | `lmp_estimate` |
| B | Due date given by her doctor | as entered | `clinician_confirmed` |
| C | Gestational age from an ultrasound (weeks + days) and the scan date | scan date + (280 − GA at scan in days) | `ultrasound` |

The LMP is also stored in `patients.lmp_date` when given.

### 5.2 Validation

| Code | Rule | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `D1` | Any date in the future except a due date | Reject with field named |
| `D2` | Resulting GA below 0 days or above 294 days (42 weeks) | Reject: ask her to check the date or talk to her doctor |
| `D3` | Ultrasound GA outside 0–42 weeks | Reject |

### 5.3 Changing dates
- The patient (or her doctor, through the Dashboard) can change dates at any time.
- A `clinician_confirmed` or `ultrasound` date replaces an `lmp_estimate` (`msg_dates_updated_by_clinician`).
- When the patient tries to replace a `clinician_confirmed` / `ultrasound` date with an LMP estimate, show `msg_confirm_override_clinician_date` and require confirmation.
- Every change is stored in `pregnancy_date_history` (section 14). Past symptom check-ins keep the pregnancy week stored when they were logged.

## 6. Calculations

### 6.1 Gestational age
```
GA_days         = 280 − (due_date − today)          # in days
completed_weeks = floor(GA_days / 7)
days            = GA_days mod 7
days_to_go      = due_date − today                  # shown only if > 0
content_week    = min(completed_weeks, 42)
```
"Today" is the patient's local date (Asia/Kolkata for v1 `[CONFIGURABLE]` `timezone`).

### 6.2 Trimester
`1` if content_week ≤ 13; `2` if 14–27; `3` if ≥ 28.

### 6.3 Pregnancy month (display only)

| Month | Content weeks | Trimester |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–4 | 1 |
| 2 | 5–8 | 1 |
| 3 | 9–13 | 1 |
| 4 | 14–17 | 2 |
| 5 | 18–22 | 2 |
| 6 | 23–27 | 2 |
| 7 | 28–31 | 3 |
| 8 | 32–35 | 3 |
| 9 | 36–42 | 3 |

There is no single standard week-to-month mapping. This table is chosen to line up with the trimester boundaries above `[CONFIRM AT REVIEW]` (R1). It lives in `config.yaml` (`month_map`), not in code.

### 6.4 Milestones (calendar markers, derived, never stored)
New week (each 7 days from GA 0), new month (first week of each month in 6.3), new trimester (weeks 14 and 28), 37 weeks (`term_from_week`, `[CONFIGURABLE]`), due date.

## 7. Illustrations and size comparison

### 7.1 Month illustrations
- 9 image files, `month_1.*` … `month_9.*` (SVG preferred; PNG or WebP allowed), taken from **publicly available drawings**. Nothing is drawn for Alovia in v1.
- **Allowed licences only:** public domain, CC0, CC BY 4.0, or CC BY-SA 4.0. Anything else (including "free to view", unclear, or non-commercial-only) is not allowed.
- **Where to look first:** womenshealth.gov drawings without a copyright mark (public domain, S1). Check the current site for a set covering the months; keep one source or one consistent style across all 9 if possible. Other openly licensed medical drawing sets may be used if they meet the licence rule.
- **Drawings only. No photographs** of embryos or fetuses.
- For each file, record in `docs/credits.md`: file name, source URL, author/publisher, licence, date downloaded, and any change made (cropping, recolouring). CC BY-SA changes must keep the CC BY-SA licence.
- Each shows a **typical** stage. Each must be reviewed by a doctor and recorded in `docs/illustration_review.md` before release. If no suitable openly licensed drawing exists for a month, show the month card without an illustration rather than use an unlicensed image.
- **Prototype builds:** placeholder images are allowed (simple shapes, or AI-generated images labelled "placeholder") only while `show_prototype_banner = true`. They must be replaced before release.
- Motion: slow float on the home screen, swipe between month cards, a gentle transition when a new week starts (Framer Motion). Respect the device "reduce motion" setting.
- Lottie: only for decorative moments (for example, a new month). Free LottieFiles animations are allowed under the Lottie Simple License (S5). Record each file used in `docs/credits.md`. **Never** shown after a pregnancy has ended.

### 7.2 Size comparison
- Shown from the `size_reference` data (section 14): for each week, a typical length and/or weight, its source, and a comparison object (icon ID + name).
- Weeks 14–40: typical weight from the WHO fetal growth charts (S3), 50th percentile. Weeks before 14: typical size from womenshealth.gov (S1), as given there.
- Weeks without a sourced value show no size comparison, never a guess.
- Comparison objects: icons from **Microsoft Fluent Emoji** only (MIT licence, S6). Include the MIT licence notice on the Credits screen and in `docs/credits.md`. Do not mix in icons from other sets. Objects familiar in India (for example mango, coconut) are preferred where the icon exists.
- Wording: "about the size of a [object]" plus the typical value. Never "your baby weighs".
- The object for each week is a doctor-reviewed choice `[CONFIRM AT REVIEW]` (R2).

## 8. Content (provider design)

### 8.1 Content item shape (the same for every provider)

```json
{
  "id": "string",
  "granularity": "month | week | week_range",
  "month": 5,
  "week_start": 20,
  "week_end": 20,
  "content_type": "fetal_development",
  "title": "string",
  "body": "string (plain text or simple Markdown)",
  "source": {
    "publisher": "string",
    "title": "string",
    "url": "string",
    "licence": "string",
    "date_accessed": "YYYY-MM-DD",
    "kb_doc_id": "string or null"
  },
  "review_status": "unreviewed | doctor_reviewed",
  "is_prototype": true
}
```

**`content_type` values:** `fetal_development`, `body_changes`, `common_symptoms`, `guidance`, `nutrition`, `dos_and_donts`, `warning_signs`, `checklist`, `discuss_with_doctor`. The final list is `[OPEN]` and can grow without code change.

**Selection rule:** for the current content week, return every item where `week_start ≤ content_week ≤ week_end`, plus month items for the current month. If both exist for the same `content_type`, show the week item on the home screen and the month item in the month detail view.

### 8.2 Content provider interface

```python
class ContentProvider(Protocol):
    def items_for_week(self, content_week: int, types: list[str] | None = None) -> list[ContentItem]: ...
    def items_for_month(self, month: int, types: list[str] | None = None) -> list[ContentItem]: ...
    def size_for_week(self, content_week: int) -> SizeReference | None: ...
```

| Provider | Reads from | When |
|---|---|---|
| `PrototypeContentProvider` | `content/prototype_content.yaml` and `content/size_reference.yaml` in the repository | Now, until the knowledge base is ready |
| `KnowledgeBaseContentProvider` | Database tables `journey_content` and `size_reference`, whose items reference `kb_documents.doc_id` | After the knowledge base and RAG build |

`config.yaml`: `content_source: prototype | knowledge_base` `[CONFIGURABLE]`. The frontend and API never know which provider is active. **Switching is a config change only.**

### 8.3 Prototype content rules
- Written only from these sources: womenshealth.gov (S1), NHS and MedlinePlus pages already in the knowledge base (Tier A), WHO growth charts (S3) for sizes only.
- Small: enough to test every screen (all 9 months, a sample of weeks, each `content_type` at least once). Not full coverage.
- Every item has a source URL and access date, `is_prototype: true`, `review_status: unreviewed`.
- Public-domain and CC BY text may be shortened to fit a card. NHS text is used only as unchanged excerpts with the knowledge base's NHS attribution, or not at all.
- While prototype content is active, `show_prototype_banner: true` shows `msg_prototype_content` on every content screen. Prototype content is not for real patients.

### 8.4 Final content rules (knowledge base)
- `journey_content` rows are written from `clean/` knowledge base files and reference their `doc_id`. Card text is shortened from public-domain or CC BY sources and approved by a doctor (`review_status = doctor_reviewed`) before release.
- Citations shown on cards are the same as the knowledge base labels (publisher, title, URL), so the Journey and the assistant cite identically.
- Only `doctor_reviewed` items are shown when `content_source = knowledge_base`.

### 8.5 Link to the assistant
When `assistant_enabled = true`, content cards show "Ask about this week", which opens the assistant with the content week set. Hidden until the RAG module is built.

## 9. Calendar, appointments and symptom integration

### 9.1 Calendar views
Month view (default) and week strip on the home screen. Navigation from the pregnancy start to the due date plus 2 weeks.

### 9.2 Markers

| Marker | Source | Look |
|---|---|---|
| New week / month / trimester, 37 weeks, due date | Section 6.4 | Small labels |
| Appointment | `appointments` | Icon + time |
| Symptom day | Module 7 `symptom_checkins` (any level) | Dot |
| Red-flag day | Check-in with level L1 or L2 | Distinct icon (never the same colour as ordinary dots) |
| Suggested check-up | Official antenatal schedule `[OPEN]` (R3) | Dashed label "suggested" |

Suggested check-ups (for example from the MCP card schedule and PMSMA) are shown **only** after the schedule is copied from an official page with URL and date in `config.yaml`. Until then this marker is disabled (`suggested_checkups_enabled: false`). **Done 2026-11-18 (owner, DEC-056):** the PMSMA fixed day (the 9th of every month in the 2nd and 3rd trimesters) was copied from the official NHM Maternal Health page with URL, exact quote and date; the MCP card gives no week-by-week visit timing. The marker is on.

Tapping a day shows that day's appointments, check-ins (with their levels) and milestones.

### 9.3 Appointments
- Entered by the patient: date, time (optional), title (max 100 characters), note (optional, max 500). Her doctor can also add appointments (Dashboard spec §16); those are marked "Added by your doctor".
- The patient can edit and delete her own appointments only, not doctor-added ones.
- Notifications in section 10.

### 9.4 Weekly summary
- "You logged": count of days with check-ins this calendar week, top symptoms by count, any red-flag days. Her data only.
- "Common at this stage": the `common_symptoms` content item for the content week (or month).
- The two parts are visually different (D15). No comparison or judgement between them ("this is normal") is ever shown.

## 11. When a pregnancy ends (birth or loss)

### 11.1 Entry
"More → My pregnancy has ended" (wording approved by the owner 2026-11-18, DEC-054). The doctor can also set it from the Dashboard.

### 11.2 Patient flow
1. Screen with calm wording and three choices: `My baby was born`, `I have lost my pregnancy`, `I prefer not to say`.
2. A question: "Would you like your doctor to be informed?" (yes / no). No default selected.
3. Confirmation.

### 11.3 What changes immediately (all choices)
- `pregnancy_status` set; `pregnancy_ended_at` stored.
- Baby card, size comparison, month cards, weekly content and `new_week` / `new_month` notifications stop.
- No celebration animation or Lottie is shown anywhere.
- Symptom history, appointments, results and doctor notes stay available under "My records".
- If she chose "yes" in step 2, the doctor gets a `pregnancy_ended` entry on the Dashboard. If "no", the status is still stored, but no notice is sent.

### 11.4 After a loss (`ended_loss`) or not specified
- A support screen (`msg_loss_support`): sympathy, that she is not alone, encouragement to talk to her doctor, and a support helpline (section 13.3).
- A safety message (`msg_after_pregnancy_danger_signs`): go to hospital for heavy bleeding, high fever or severe pain. Based on the MCP card danger signs after delivery (S4) `[CONFIRM AT REVIEW]` (R4).
- Home screen becomes a quiet page with links to "My records", doctor notes and support. No baby imagery.

### 11.5 After a birth (`ended_birth`)
- A short, warm message (`msg_birth_congratulations`) and the same safety message as 11.4.
- Note that postpartum features will come in a later version.

### 11.6 Undo
"I marked this by mistake" is available for `resume_window_days` (7, `[CONFIGURABLE]`): it restores `active` and logs the change in `pregnancy_date_history`.

## 13. Wording

### 13.1 Rules
- Always "typical", "about", "estimated". Never "your baby is/weighs".
- Never say a symptom is normal or nothing to worry about.
- Educational content shows its source on the card.
- After a pregnancy ends, no cheerful or celebratory wording anywhere except `msg_birth_congratulations`.

### 13.2 Message keys (intent only; final text in `messages.yaml`, doctor-approved)

| Key | Intent |
|---|---|
| `msg_onboarding` | Enter your dates so we can show your journey |
| `msg_estimate_label` | This date is an estimate from your last period; your doctor may confirm a different date |
| `msg_dates_updated_by_clinician` | Your doctor's date is now used |
| `msg_confirm_override_clinician_date` | Your current date was set by your doctor or a scan; are you sure you want to change it? |
| `msg_past_due_date` | You are past your estimated due date; stay in touch with your doctor |
| `msg_beyond_42_weeks` | Please contact your doctor |
| `msg_typical_not_yours` | Pictures and sizes show a typical baby; every pregnancy is different |
| `msg_prototype_content` | Sample content for testing; not reviewed by a doctor |
| `msg_loss_support` | Sympathy; you are not alone; talk to your doctor; support helpline |
| `msg_after_pregnancy_danger_signs` | Go to hospital for heavy bleeding, high fever or severe pain |
| `msg_birth_congratulations` | Warm congratulations; postpartum features come later |

### 13.3 Phone numbers
Any helpline or emergency number is `[OPEN]` until copied from an official Government of India or Telangana page, with URL and date recorded in `messages.yaml`. The same numbers are used by Module 7. **Done 2026-11-18 (owner, DEC-057):** "Ambulance | 102,108" and "Women Helpline | 181", copied word for word from the official Telangana State Portal emergency contacts page; it lists no counselling or mental-health line.

## 15. Tests (all must pass before release)

1. GA calculation for options A, B and C, including day boundaries and the patient timezone.
2. Validation D1–D3.
3. Month mapping at every boundary in section 6.3; trimester at 13/14 and 27/28.
4. States: no date, 39 weeks, 40 weeks (past-due message), 42+ weeks (counter stops).
5. Switching `content_source` between `prototype` and `knowledge_base` returns the same item shape and the same screens work.
6. With `content_source = knowledge_base`, `unreviewed` items are never returned.
7. Weeks without a size reference show no size comparison.
8. Calendar shows symptom dots and distinct red-flag icons from Module 7 data.
9. Pregnancy end: all three choices stop baby content and weekly notifications; no Lottie plays; doctor informed only when "yes"; undo works within 7 days and is logged.
10. Dates changed by a clinician cannot be silently overwritten by an LMP estimate.
11. No screen uses the words "your baby weighs" or shows a heartbeat animation (manual check).

## 17. Out of scope (version 1) and Do not

**Out of scope:** postpartum features; push notifications; multiple-pregnancy (twins) content; weekly illustrations; 3D or Rive animations; kick counting; AI-generated content; partner or family accounts; offline mode.

**Do not:**
- Do not write medical content from memory or generate it with AI.
- Do not show prototype content without the banner.
- Do not show baby content after a pregnancy has ended.
- Do not animate a heartbeat.
- Do not put content or the month mapping directly in code.
- Do not show a size without a source.
- Do not enable suggested check-ups before the official schedule is copied.
